BEFORE YOU DIG. Positions come from a photo survey, not a measured one. Set out every pot, walk the beds, then dig.
Two numbers per pot: metres along the wall from the house end, then metres out from the wall. Tick each pot once it is set out. Ticks are saved on this phone only.
Three things the plan depends on
  1. Cut the Buddleja by the terrace (17) and the tall Buddleja (21) down to about 60 cm every March, or the plants in front of them will end up underneath.
  2. Keep the big silver Elaeagnus (07) trimmed back to its present line on the path side; the Artemisia next to it need the sun.
  3. Test the soil with a pH kit before planting the Skimmia and Liriope. If it reads above 7, plant them in ericaceous compost.
